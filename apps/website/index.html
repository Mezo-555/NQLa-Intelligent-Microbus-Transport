What NQLa Delivers

A complete AI-driven ecosystem for Alexandria's microbus network — built for passengers, drivers, and transit administrators.

🗺️

Route & Multi-Transfer Planning

Find the best combination of microbus lines across Alexandria stops — powered by PostGIS spatial queries and graph routing.

📡

Real-Time Ride Matching

Live GPS streams from driver smartphones via WebSocket — no OBD hardware, no extra cost per vehicle.

🧠

Egyptian Arabic NLP

Understands Egyptian dialect and Franco-Arabic passenger feedback — sentiment, complaints, and safety concerns auto-classified.

📊

Demand Forecasting & Incentives

Spatiotemporal ML models predict demand surges by zone and time, then push dynamic incentives to balance driver supply.

🛡️

Driver Safety Scoring

Continuous safety score per driver based on trip data, complaint classification, and behavioral pattern detection.

🤖

Transportation AI Agent

LLM-powered agent with verified route retrieval, forecasting tools, and strict safety guardrails for passengers and admins.
